## Corpus decisions

1. The three EU forward rows may enter the corpus at **H** only with the corrected wording above.
2. Germany and Spain require mandatory pinpoint corrections before ingestion.
3. France requires ADEME/SYDEREP—not CITEO—to be stored as the register authority.
4. Italy must store separate fields for **EPR compliance route (CONAI/autonomous system)** and **national register framework/status (RENAP)**.
5. The Netherlands must use **Verpact** as the current operator name and must not treat the historic 50-tonne fee/reporting threshold as a PPWR Article 44 registration exemption.
6. Poland’s existing BDO basis is confirmed; proposed national PPWR implementation must remain a monitored future-law record.

## EU baseline for every Member-State row

For PPWR EPR purposes, the common Union obligation is [Regulation (EU) 2025/40, Article 44](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2025/40/oj/eng): a producer must register in each Member State where it first makes packaging or packaged products available, subject to the PPWR producer definition and the permitted use of a producer-responsibility organisation. The Commission’s current interpretation confirms that the producer registers/reports and pays EPR fees in the Member State where the packaging is expected to become waste: [Commission Guidance C/2026/3084](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX:52026XC03084).
